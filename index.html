// 05 beyond the blueprint
“Let’s talk apart from work — do you have a favourite song, or a coffee spot you always go back to? I had one.”

My journey didn't begin in a lab — it began in the lush, rain-drenched hills of the Western Ghats in India, where the monsoons set the calendar and summer meant climbing trees for sun-ripened mangoes. My school felt more like a home than a classroom. Time moved the way it should.

Manufacturing was always in my blood. I grew up watching sparks fly in our family's workshop — wood shavings, steel frames, things being made with hands. But I felt a pull toward something else: I wanted to find the brain behind the brawn of the machines. That question carried me from the fast streets of Pune all the way to Germany.

The path wasn't always smooth. Just as I was finding my footing in Mechatronics, the world stopped for a pandemic. It tested my limits — but it also sharpened my focus. Today, as a Senior Engineer at Deloitte, that question finally has an answer: I connect the Shop Floor to the Top Floor.
